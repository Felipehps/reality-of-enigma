## Prompt utilizado
Create a production game environment backdrop texture for a playable Unreal Engine dark fantasy boss arena. Use the supplied image ONLY as art-direction reference: gothic infernal collapsing ruins, black obsidian, crimson and violet sky, fire in distant towers, ash, sinister monumental arches. Landscape wide panoramic matte painting, extremely detailed realistic painterly fantasy, cinematic atmospheric depth. Environment ONLY, absolutely NO characters, NO boss, NO heroes, NO swords, NO health bars, NO UI, NO typography, NO logos, NO foreground ability effects, NO central black orb, NO eclipsed sun (the game renders its eclipse separately). Composition: near-dark basalt ruined silhouettes along lower third; richly textured charcoal crimson storm clouds above, distant immense gothic citadel burning through lava cracks across horizon, scattered distant airborne rubble. A broad quiet central gap for silhouettes of moving 3D actors, towers arranged mainly toward the sides. Horizon level and centered perspective, no dutch angle. Bright distant lava fire accents against dark ruins but do not wash out or overexpose. Fill the entire rectangular frame edge to edge. This is a background texture asset, not a screenshot of a finished game.
